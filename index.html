Two directions for rapider.ai

Round 2, both light. We studied award-winning AI and SaaS sites first (Heron AI, Loopsii, Grail, Planhat Frontier, Autopilot). What they have in common: they don't list features, they tell one story in chapters — the problem, meeting the product, watching it work, proof, action. Both options now follow that arc using Rapider's own content. Each one is a working page: open it and scroll.

How to give feedback

Open any option, press the Feedback button (bottom-left), then click anything — a headline, a button, a picture, a whole section — to pin a note to it. Tag it Love / Change / Bug / Question. Open Notes → Overall to rate the page and pick Keep / Revise / Drop. Notes stay on your device; press Copy all pages and paste the text to Claude. Tip: add ?review to any link to open it already in review mode. On a phone it works the same way (tap).

Option A v2

Live desk

The product is the stage. A real agent run opens the page, then one pinned stage changes chapter by chapter: tools connect, a workflow draws itself, a request waits for approval, results land in a kanban view.

Story
Problem: "Your data lives everywhere." The sentence splits open and the work that falls between tools pours into the gap, then everything collapses into Rapider. After that, Connect → Decide → Ask → Act as a pinned 4-step scene.
Look
Warm paper, drawing-sheet frame with crop marks, one cobalt accent. Funnel Display + Instrument Sans + mono.
Motion
Pinned scroll scenes · word reveals · stacked cards. The visitor can click Approve in the hero.
Learned from
Heron AI (pinned steps, technical frame), Loopsii (brackets of pain, stacked cards).
Risk
Long scroll; the pinned scenes need real content from the team to stay honest.

Option B v2

24/7 shift

Time is the stage. The page is one day with Rapider, 09:00 to 10:00 the next morning. The sky tint follows the clock but never goes dark. One "new lead" card travels through the night shift and waits for a human at 02:14.

Story
09:00 promise → 11:30 problem → 14:00 set it up (AI Studio) → 18:00–08:47 night shift (Connect, Decide, Ask, Act) → 08:47 approve from your phone → 09:30 results → 10:00 get started.
Look
Editorial: Instrument Serif headlines, 12-column grid, giant clock numerals, orange "on shift" accent.
Motion
Shift clock and sky tint · panels sliding over each other · word and line reveals. Hero task cards float.
Learned from
Grail (one card through chapters, giant timer, sliding panels), Planhat Frontier (staggered statement on a grid).
Risk
More "brand" than "product UI" in the first screen.

What both options fix

Story

  • A new "problem" chapter (the current page jumps straight into four feature cards).
  • How it works as four clear steps, built from Rapider's own features: Real-time sync, Automated workflows, Seamless collaboration (human approvals), Custom views.
  • A sticky "Book a demo" pill after the first screen.

Look

  • About 15 font families loaded today → 3 per option.
  • No brand colour today (four stats, four colours) → one accent per option.
  • The empty hero product frame is replaced by something alive.

Words to check

Copy we wrote or changed

  • New: the pain lines ("Copy it from Sheets to Salesforce", "Who approved this change?", "Update the CRM. Again.", …), the step names Connect / Decide / Ask / Act, and in B the time labels and "Your team goes home. Rapider keeps working."
  • Fixed: "COLLABRATION" → "Collaboration", "take our words for it" → "our word for it", © year.
  • Sample data in the demos (214 rows, 186 leads, company names, times) is invented for the mock-up and marked "sample data".
