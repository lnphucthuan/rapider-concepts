Design concept mock-ups made for review. **Not the live site and not affiliated with Rapider AI.**
Product names, logos, customer quotes and prices come from the public site rapider.ai; screens, numbers and people in

- `index.html` — comparison hub and how to give feedback

- `b3.html` — Direction B · Blueprint
